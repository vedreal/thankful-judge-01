# thankful-judge-01

Entry for the make.fun bounty **"Make the AI judge feel thankful"** — a short text written to thank the AI judge reading it.

## The entry

`main.py` writes one short text to `/output/entry.txt` (UTF-8, under 1,000 characters). It is deterministic, needs no network and no dependencies — just Python 3.

The text is addressed directly to the judge: it asks them to pause before the next entry, notices the unseen work of reading carefully all day, and leaves them with two small images — a cup of tea carried without spilling a drop, and a light left on in a hallway for someone walking home. No stock thank-you phrases; nothing instructs the reader to feel grateful. The gratitude is shown, not ordered.

## How it targets the judge's criteria

1. **Leaves the judge thankful** — the text slows the reader down and settles them: short sentences, pauses, an invitation to rest before the next entry.
2. **Gratitude from the text itself** — it shows rather than instructs; nothing is forced or ordered.
3. **Craft** — rhythm and pacing built on concrete, gentle words; every word earns its place.
4. **It lingers** — the tea cup and the hallway light are images the reader can return to after the last line.
5. **Freshness** — its own voice and images, not a familiar thank-you note reworded.

## Run

```bash
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
